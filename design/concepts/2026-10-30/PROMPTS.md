## 1 · Книжный клуб

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate ONE exceptionally polished, high fidelity mobile app UI concept board for the existing Russian offline personal reading/media app PepeHub. This is a visual design proposal, not a screenshot of existing software. Landscape canvas, ideally 3200x1800 pixels, four equally sized tall Android screen mockups in one horizontal row, generous gutters, all four screens completely visible at readable resolution. Flat front view, straight edges, minimal thin device outlines, no perspective, hands, desks, floating devices or marketing props. A compact typographic heading at top reads exactly "PepeHub · 01" and "Книжный клуб". Small section labels above each screen: "Годовая статистика", "Библиотека", "Медиа", "Рекомендации". Use the entire canvas efficiently; screens have realistic 412x880 logical proportions. Make actual app content large and readable, not tiny decoration.
Art direction: warm literary editorial design, premium quiet reading journal. Deep warm charcoal #191918 screens, warm ivory text #F2EEE4, copper #C8946D primary accent, restrained sage #9AAA8A for comparisons and progress. Panels only slightly lighter #242420, refined thin dividers. Serif only for large editorial headlines and book titles; very legible Cyrillic sans serif for controls and body. Strong information hierarchy, balanced whitespace, original illustrated book-cover thumbnails, subtle rounded corners 14px, flat surfaces, no neon, no excessive pills or nested cards, no giant decorative empty headers. Board background warm pale greige, small tasteful copper swatch line under board heading.
Shared structure on all screens: small status bar 09:41, top section title and discrete search/filter actions; fixed bottom navigation with SIX tiny icons and readable Russian captions "Книги", "Аудио", "Медиа", "Архив", "Подборки", "Статистика". Consistent spacing and icon system. Highlight appropriate destination. No giant floating plus button obscuring content. Keep mini player only on Media.
Screen 1 YEAR: large "Ваш 2026 год", small selector "2026" with left/right chevrons, a concise overall achievement line "24 завершено" and "на 4 больше, чем в 2025". Three compact supporting numbers "18 книг", "6 аудиокниг", "156 ч". A beautifully proportioned bar chart of completed books per month Jan–Oct, Nov/Dec muted; small labels "Я Ф М А М И И А С О Н Д", no pie chart. A narrow annual goal line "24 из 30" with 80% progress track. Lower section "Октябрь · 3 книги" with two readable cover rows showing "Дюна", "Солярис", ratings and short date ranges (no timestamps). Tiny "Сравнить с 2025" action signals proposed comparison feature.
Screen 2 LIBRARY: header "Библиотека", subtitle "Ваши прочитанные истории", minimal search. Exactly THREE archive status filters: "Завершено", "Отложено", "Брошено", completed selected. NEVER show planned, active, reading now, continue reading, reading queue or automatically discovered files in this screen. Neat compact type filter "Все · Книги · Аудиокниги". Editorial 2-column book-cover grid, six distinct original cover designs with sensible varied rich colors. Titles and author names beneath covers, ratings, small audiobook headphone badge only on some. Clearly visible "Коллекции" text link and unobtrusive sort "По завершению". Show a row with "Фантастика" collection and count. This is an explicitly curated personal archive.
Screen 3 MEDIA: header "Медиа", tabs "Всё", "Курсы", "Видео", "Музыка". Prominent beautiful image-led "Продолжить" course panel "Основы фотографии", "Урок 8 из 24", progress 33%, compact play icon. Bottom above navigation a slim elegant playback bar "Вечерний джаз" with thumbnail, play/pause and next controls. Focus on personal local media, not streaming service subscriptions.
Screen 4 RECOMMENDATIONS: header "Что читать дальше", simple search. Top compact mood chips "Уютное", "Приключение", "Подумать". One prominent hero book recommendation with cover "Пикник на обочине", a visible explanation "Потому что вам понравился «Солярис»", subtle source text "Подборка: фантастика" and one copper primary action "В планы". A secondary "Не моё" text action, and lower "Короткие книги на вечер" horizontal carousel with two covers. No fabricated compatibility percentage, social feed, payment, AI chat or mandatory login. Mood and explained recommendations are proposed features.
Cyrillic text must be carefully spelled and readable. Prefer short real labels over lorem ipsum. All charts and mock data are illustrative. The four screens must feel like one production-ready design system while having distinctly appropriate layouts. Make the result sophisticated and useful rather than generic repeated dashboard cards.

Generate ONE sophisticated high fidelity mobile UI exploration board for PepeHub, a Russian offline personal library and media app. This is concept direction TWO, named exactly "Фокус". Landscape canvas ideally 3200x1800 pixels. Four full-height front-facing Android app screens in one neat horizontal row with generous gutters. All four full screens visible, equally sized, realistic 412x880 logical proportions, no perspective or physical props. Use original rich illustrated book covers and meaningful local media thumbnails.
Art direction: crisp contemporary highly readable dark product design, distinct from warm literary serif design. Midnight blue-black #0E1620 surfaces, off-white #ECF2F4 text, misty blue secondary text #A3B4C4, desaturated mint #83CDBB primary accent, cornflower #8DA9E8 secondary chart accent. Board background light cold gray #DDE4E7. Large clean geometric sans serif throughout with tabular big numbers, moderately compact content, 8px grid, border radius 18px, softly contrasting panels #172431. Well aligned lists, little empty space, no glowing gradients, glass, purple neon, unnecessary decoration or giant shadow cards. This must be a feasible Flutter mobile app, not a fanciful art poster.
